## 📌 Overview

This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Court Case Management Systems (CMS)**. These systems empower courts, clerks, judges, prosecutors, public defenders, probation departments, and justice agencies to manage cases from initial filing through hearings, orders, disposition, financial processing, and archival.

Modern court case-management platforms provide:
- 🏛️ **Court Administration & Dockets**: Case registration, party management, hearings, courtroom scheduling, and dockets.
- 📜 **E-Filing & Document Ingestion**: Digital submission, OCR, judicial verification, electronic service, and statutory fee calculations.
- ⚖️ **Judicial Workflows & Caseflow**: Bench queues, orders, warrants, differentiated case management, and disposition tracking.
- 💳 **Court Financials & Accounting**: Fines, restitution, bail/bonds, fee disbursement, and general ledger reconciliation.
- 🔍 **Discovery, AI & Public Portals**: Public record search, access control, redaction, and judicial analytics.
